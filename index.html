✨ What Playport Does For You

Playport is a simple idea with a big payoff: your iPhone becomes the computer. Instead of streaming a game from a remote machine or keeping a PC switched on in another room, Playport takes a game from your own library, puts it onto your phone, and runs it there using the phone's own processor and graphics chip.

That means a few very practical things for you as a player:

  • No second computer needed. Nothing has to be running at home while you play.
  • No internet connection needed to play. Once a game is installed on the phone, the heavy lifting happens in your hand.
  • No waiting on a stream. There is no video being piped across the network, so there is nothing to buffer or disconnect.
  • No jailbreak required. Playport works on a stock iPhone, exactly as it came out of the box.
In one sentence: Install a game, pick up a controller, press Play, and the game runs on your phone's own hardware.

🚀 Getting Started

Getting up and running does not require any technical knowledge. There is no command line, no configuration file, and no developer tool to install. You simply download the application, open it, and follow the on-screen steps.

Here is the whole journey in plain language:

  • Step 1 — Download. Visit the download link and get the latest release of Playport.
  • Step 2 — Open Playport. Launch the application and let it finish setting itself up the first time it runs.
  • Step 3 — Add a game to your library. Point Playport at the game you want to bring over. It will show up in your library once it is ready.
  • Step 4 — Install it onto your iPhone. Choose the game in your library and install it onto the phone. This step takes a little time, so let it finish without closing anything.
  • Step 5 — Connect a controller and press Play. Pair your controller, open the game from your library, and press Play. That is it.

Everything after Step 5 is just gaming. You can return to your library at any time to launch another game you have already installed.

🕹️ Playing With A Controller

Playport is built around a controller-first experience. Once your controller is connected to your iPhone, you can browse your library, launch a game, and play without touching the screen.

  • Pair once, play often. After the first pairing, your controller reconnects on its own.
  • Comfortable on the small screen. A physical controller keeps the game's visuals unobstructed by on-screen buttons.
  • Made for long sessions. No touch controls means no accidental taps during a critical moment.

If a controller does not respond, switch it off and on again, then reopen the game from your library. In almost every case, that is all it takes.

💻 What You Will Need

Playport is deliberately undemanding. To get the best experience, check the following before you begin:

  • An iPhone that has not been jailbroken. A stock device is exactly what Playport is designed for.
  • A Windows computer. This is where you download Playport and manage the games in your library.
  • A game you already own. Playport runs games from your own library; it does not sell or supply them.
  • Some free storage on the phone. Installed games live on the phone, so leave room for them.
  • A compatible controller. Any controller that pairs with your iPhone will do.

The first game you install will be the slowest step, because Playport has to prepare things from scratch. Later installs are quicker.

🧩 32-bit And 64-bit Games

Playport supports both 64-bit and 32-bit Windows games. The 32-bit support is a recent addition, which means a much larger part of older game libraries can now be brought over to the phone.

  • 64-bit games are the modern standard, and Playport has supported them from the start.
  • 32-bit games cover a huge number of classics and older titles that were previously out of reach.

If a game from your library does not behave as expected, try launching it again. Many small hiccups clear themselves up on a second attempt.

❓ Common Questions

Do I need a PC running while I play?

No. The game runs on your phone's own CPU and GPU. There is no PC, no cloud and no stream involved in playing.

Do I need to jailbreak my iPhone?

No. Playport runs on a stock, non-jailbroken iPhone.

Will my game progress and settings be preserved?

Games are installed onto the phone as complete packages, so whatever you set up when you add them to your library carries over.

How do I get updates?

Visit the download page again and download the newest release. Updates use the same simple process as your first download.

What if the game will not start?

Close the game, reopen Playport, and press Play again. If it still will not start, make sure the install onto the phone finished completely before you launched it.
